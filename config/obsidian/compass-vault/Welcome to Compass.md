# Welcome to Compass 🧭

Welcome to your personal navigation system! Compass is here to help you find direction through journaling, reflection, and AI-assisted insights.

## Getting Started

### 1. Daily Journaling
Create a new journal entry in the `Journal/` folder. Use the templates in `Templates/` to get started.

### 2. Set Your Telos
Telos is your "end goal" - what you're working toward. Open [[Telos]] to define yours.

### 3. Explore the System
- **Journal/** - Your daily entries
- **Templates/** - Pre-made templates for journal entries
- **This note** - Getting started guide (you're here!)

## What Makes Compass Different?

- **Privacy-first:** All your data stays on your machine
- **AI-assisted:** (Coming soon) Get insights and reflections from AI
- **Progressive:** Start simple, reveal complexity as you explore
- **Flexible:** Use it via this app, mobile, or even text messaging
