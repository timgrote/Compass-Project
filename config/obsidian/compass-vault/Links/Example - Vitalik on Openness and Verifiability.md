# Vitalik on Openness and Verifiability

**URL**: https://vitalik.eth.limo/general/2025/09/24/openness_and_verifiability.html

**Shared by**: Hunter

**Date**: 2025-10-08

## Why This Is Relevant

Core principle for our [[Ideas/Verification Agent]] work - "verify, don't trust" applied beyond just crypto. Vitalik argues that openness and verifiability should extend across software, hardware, and even biology.

Connects to Hunter's focus on data ownership and Tiny Cloud protocol - civilizations that produce technology (vs. just consume it) gain power and independence.

## Key Takeaways

- **Openness alone isn't enough**: Open source can still be unverifiable if it's too complex to audit
- **Verifiability requires**:
  - Transparency (can inspect)
  - Comprehensibility (can understand)
  - Reproducibility (can rebuild and verify)
- **Civilizational stakes**: Societies that depend on black-box tech become vulnerable
- **Beyond software**: Same principles should apply to hardware (chip design), biology (genetic engineering), infrastructure
